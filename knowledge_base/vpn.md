# VPN Authentication Failure

## Scope

Use this procedure when an employee reports that the corporate VPN will not connect or reports an authentication error.

## User-safe troubleshooting

1. Close the VPN client completely and reopen it.
2. Confirm that the employee can sign in to Microsoft 365 using their normal work account.
3. Ask the employee for the exact VPN error message.
4. Ask whether the issue occurs on more than one network.

## Technician actions

1. Verify the user's account is active and not locked.
2. Verify required VPN group membership.
3. Review VPN client logs if available.
4. Confirm the device meets company access requirements.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- the account is disabled;
- required permissions are missing;
- multiple employees report the same VPN issue;
- the VPN infrastructure appears unavailable;
- Tier 1 troubleshooting does not resolve the issue.
