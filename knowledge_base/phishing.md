# Suspected Phishing Email

## Scope

Use this procedure when an employee reports a suspicious email, unexpected login request, suspicious attachment, or unexpected link.

## User-safe actions

1. Do not click links or open attachments in the suspicious message.
2. Do not reply to the sender.
3. Report the message using the company's approved phishing reporting process.
4. Ask the employee whether they clicked a link, opened an attachment, or entered credentials.

## Technician actions

1. Record the sender, subject, and reported time of the message.
2. Determine whether the employee interacted with the message.
3. Preserve relevant information required by the security response process.

## Escalation

Escalate to the security team when:

- credentials were entered;
- an attachment was opened;
- a suspicious link was accessed;
- multiple employees received the message;
- compromise is suspected.

Follow company security incident procedures for confirmed or suspected compromise.
