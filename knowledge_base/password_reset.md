# Password Reset / Account Lockout

## Scope

Use this procedure when an employee reports that they forgot their password, cannot sign in, or their company account is locked after multiple failed login attempts.

## User-safe troubleshooting

1. Ask the employee to stop repeated sign-in attempts temporarily if the account appears locked.
2. Confirm the exact error message displayed.
3. Ask whether the employee can sign in to other company services.
4. Confirm whether the issue affects only the employee's account.

## Technician actions

1. Verify the employee's identity using the approved identity verification procedure.
2. Check whether the account is locked, disabled, or requires a password reset.
3. Review recent failed authentication attempts when available.
4. Follow the approved account unlock or password reset procedure.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- identity cannot be verified;
- suspicious authentication activity is present;
- the account repeatedly locks after being restored;
- the account is disabled for an unknown reason;
- Tier 1 troubleshooting does not resolve the issue.
